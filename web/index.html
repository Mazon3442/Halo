Looks

Import a .cube LUT from Premiere, Resolve, Final Cut or CapCut, or a Lightroom preset (.xmp, curves included). Export LUT saves this grade as a .cube to use in those apps.

Film

Print film curves. Small differences between their red, green and blue responses give film its color: cooler shadows and warmer highlights, without a tint over the picture.

Color wheels

Drag a wheel's dot toward a color to push the shadows, midtones or highlights that way. The slider under each wheel brightens or darkens that range. Double-click a wheel to reset it. Protect skin keeps faces out of the wheels' color.

Curves

Click the line to add a point and drag it. Drag a point off the graph, or double-click it, to remove it. The faint shape behind is this frame's histogram.

HSL

Change one color at a time, like Lightroom's HSL panel: skies bluer, foliage less green, skin a touch lighter.
